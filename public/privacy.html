Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Your privacy and digital rights are fundamental to our architecture.

Zero Storage Commitment

YT Download does not require user registration or logins. We do not store, archive, or retain any media streams, video URLs, or download logs on permanent disks. Once a download stream is transferred to your device, it leaves our temporary volatile memory entirely.

1. Information We Collect (And Do Not Collect)

  • No Personal Profiles: We do not ask for your name, phone number, physical address, or payment details.
  • No File Logging: We do not log which individual video URLs you process.
  • Server Health Logs: Automated web server requests may record temporary diagnostic data (such as IP addresses, timestamps, and browser user agents) in RAM to prevent denial-of-service abuse and maintain server uptime. These temporary logs are automatically cleared.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We use standard Google Analytics (`gtag.js`) to measure aggregated, anonymized metrics such as page visits and country-level traffic. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site trackers or sell data to advertising brokers.

3. Security Practices

All communication between your browser and our servers is strictly encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption. Modern security headers (`X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options`, and `Referrer-Policy`) are enforced on every request.
