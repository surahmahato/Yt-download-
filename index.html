Under the Hood: How the Extraction Engine Operates

Understanding the modern stream delivery pipeline powering sub-second video analysis

Manifest Parsing & Decryption

When you submit a URL, our backend negotiates with CDN endpoints to parse DASH, HLS, and progressive MP4 manifests. For platforms using dynamic cipher signatures, our extraction pipeline deciphers stream tokens on-the-fly to retrieve valid video URLs.

Byte-Stream Chunked Pipelining

Rather than saving entire multi-gigabyte video files to server hard drives, our architecture uses Node.js HTTP stream piping. Content chunks stream directly from CDN nodes to the user's browser, eliminating storage bottlenecks and ensuring rapid download speeds.

Audio Demuxing & Codec Transcoding

For MP3 conversions, the audio stream is isolated from the container. The demuxer strips video tracks and packages the audio with ID3 metadata tags, providing 320kbps MP3 files that seamlessly integrate with Android and iOS music players.
