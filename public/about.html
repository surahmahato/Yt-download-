About YT Download

YT Download is a free, high-performance web and Android media utility engineered to simplify online video archiving and format conversion for content creators, researchers, and everyday viewers.

Our Mission

We believe accessing public media content across devices should be seamless, private, and accessible without intrusive adware or bloated software. Our engine extracts clean direct stream links with zero quality degradation, delivering crisp 1080p MP4 video and 320kbps MP3 audio directly to your device storage.

Core Capabilities & Supported Formats

YouTube & Shorts

Full HD 1080p, 720p HD, 480p, and high-bitrate studio-grade 320kbps MP3 audio conversion for offline listening.

TikTok (No Watermark)

Clean extraction of original TikTok videos without floating logos or watermarks, preserving full framerate and sound.

Instagram Reels & Posts

Instant extraction of high-resolution Reels, stories, and carousel photos directly into your phone gallery.

Architecture & Security

YT Download functions strictly as a client-initiated pass-through proxy utility. We do not store, archive, or host media files on our infrastructure. All downloads are generated on-the-fly and streamed directly to your browser's download manager or Android Gallery.
